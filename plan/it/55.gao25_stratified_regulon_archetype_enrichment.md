## What exists now

`42`–`45` are the gao25 analogue of `41`: four near-identical scripts (309–312 lines each,
**1239 lines total**), one per layer, each a copy of 41 with the regulon table swapped for
`links/it/regulon_gene_table_gao25_v1<token>.csv`. Each carries its **own verbatim copy** of
`reconstruct_gene_universe` — they import nothing from 41, which is why 41's recent
`(universe, stats)` refactor did not touch them.

They use the same one-stratum Fisher null 41 does, and so inherit both of the inflations 54
documents: the null ignores that markers and regulon targets are each biased toward highly
expressed genes, and the Haldane–Anscombe odds ratio overstates the enrichment ratio at small
counts.

## The key structural fact

Only the **regulon source** differs between 41 and 42–45. The gene universe, the archetype
marker sets and therefore the expression strata are *identical* to 54's — same h5ads, same
coords, same `3X.follow.*_archetype_markers.tsv`. So 54's machinery applies unchanged, and 55
is 54 with one loader swapped, not a reimplementation.

## Does the result reproduce on gao25? Yes — measured

Prototyped end-to-end against the real tables (54's `make_strata` / `stratified_null`,
41's universe, gao25 regulons parsed by 42's rule):

| layer | N | gao25 regulons | pairs | overlap=0 | overlap>=5 | med log2_or | med log2_enr | universe shift `log2_or` | universe shift `log2_enr` | ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L4 | 16551 | 78 | 234 | 65.0% | 20 | +1.747 | −0.242 | **+0.939** | **−0.008** | 120× |
| L2/3 | 16544 | 77 | 231 | 51.5% | 35 | +1.178 | −0.343 | **+0.984** | **+0.049** | 20× |
| L5IT | 16338 | 52 | 104 | 40.4% | 22 | +0.965 | −0.254 | **+0.972** | **+0.094** | 10× |
| L6IT | 16443 | 48 | 144 | 53.5% | 11 | +1.536 | −0.175 | **+0.944** | **−0.038** | 25× |

("universe shift" = median change in the statistic when `U` is swapped from the expressed-gene
universe to the union of that layer's regulon targets — the test that motivated 54.)

Three things to note before writing any code:

1. **The finding transfers.** `log2_or` carries a +0.94 to +0.98 universe-dependent offset on
   gao25; `log2_enr` moves by −0.04 to +0.09. 10–120× more stable, same as on yoo25.
2. **The offset is smaller than yoo25's** (+0.94–0.98 vs +1.46–2.09), because gao25's regulons
   cover a different share of the universe. That is a reason to re-derive thresholds for gao25
   rather than inherit 54b's, not a reason to skip the correction.
3. **gao25 has far fewer regulons than yoo25 in the upper layers** — L2/3 77 vs 139, L4 78 vs
   107 — and comparable numbers below (L5IT 52 vs 54, L6IT 48 vs 49). Every gao25 regulon
   clears `MIN_REGULON_GENES = 5`, so nothing is dropped. Expect a shorter figure than 54b's.

## Design

### One script, not four

`55.gao25_stratified_regulon_archetype_enrichment.py`, handling all four layers through a
`LAYERS` config exactly as `54` does. `42`–`45` split by layer purely because they were written
as four copies of a single-layer script; there is no per-layer logic that justifies it, and
consolidating removes ~900 lines of duplication rather than adding another 1200.

Reuse, via the `importlib` pattern already used across the family:

- from `41`: `reconstruct_gene_universe`, `LAYERS`, `DATASETS`, `log2_odds_ratio`,
  `MIN_REGULON_GENES`, `ARCHETYPE_LETTERS`
- from `54`: `make_strata`, `stratified_null`, `tail_and_midp`, `score_layer`, `decorate`,
  `mc_check`, `universe_robustness`, `bin_sensitivity`, `N_BINS`, `BIN_COVARIATE`

The only genuinely new code is the gao25 regulon loader (~10 lines, lifted from `42:161-168`)
and the `LAYERS`-to-token mapping (`L2_3→l23, L4→l4, L5IT→l5, L6IT→l6`).

**`54` needs one small change to make this possible:** `build_layer` currently hard-codes
reading `cfg['regulons']` in 41's format. Lift the regulon loading into a callable passed in —
`build_layer(cfg, m41, adatas, load_regulons=...)` — so 55 supplies the gao25 parser. Nothing
else in 54 changes, and its outputs must still reproduce byte-for-byte afterwards.

### Do NOT duplicate 54b–54e

This is the decision that matters, and the one 42–45 got wrong at smaller scale. The four
figure scripts are pure-plotting and already ~900 lines; copying them to `55b`–`55e` with an
input prefix swapped would recreate exactly the duplication this plan is removing from the
compute side, and guarantee the two families drift.

**Recommended:** parameterise. Give each of `54b`/`54c`/`54d`/`54e` a config dict at module top —

```python
CFG_YOO25 = dict(tag='yoo25', res_prefix='54', fig_prefix='54b',
                 native_tmpl='54.{layer}_stratified_enrichment.tsv',
                 l23set='54.l23set_stratified_enrichment.tsv',
                 title='mouse IT subclasses')
```

— and change `main()` to `main(cfg=CFG_YOO25)`. Then `55b`–`55e` are ~25-line entry points that
define `CFG_GAO25` and call `m54b.main(CFG_GAO25)`. Same file count, same numbering convention,
one copy of the logic.

**Fallback if that refactor is unwanted:** write only `55` plus a single `55b` that emits the
gao25 heatmap, and skip the PDF/dot-plot derivatives for gao25 until they are actually needed.
Do not copy all four.

### Thresholds must be re-derived, not inherited

`54b` uses `STAR_LOG2ENR = 1.0` and ramp `[-1.5, 3.5]`, both picked off the yoo25 selection
curve and the yoo25 observed range. gao25's `log2_enr` distribution differs (its universe
offset is ~half yoo25's), so 55b must re-run that derivation: tabulate how many cells survive at
several cutoffs, pick the shoulder, and set the ramp from gao25's own observed min/max over
unmasked activating cells. `MASK_MIN_OVERLAP = 5` stays — the one criterion no universe or null
choice can inflate.

Note also that `42`–`45` already diverge from 41 on the naive side (`LOG2OR_SHOW = 3.0` vs 2.0,
`STAR_FDR = 1e-2` vs 1e-5), so there is no single inherited convention to fall back on.

## Two pre-existing issues to settle while here

1. **`is_extended` is handled differently in the two datasets, so a regulon name does not mean
   the same thing.** `40` (yoo25) keeps a regulon's *direct* targets if it exists in both, else
   its extended targets. `42`–`45` (gao25) **ignore `is_extended` entirely** and union direct +
   extended for the same TF + sign. So `Rfx3_+/+` is a different gene set in the two families.
   This does not invalidate either analysis internally, but it silently weakens any
   yoo25↔gao25 comparison — which is the main reason to run 55 at all.

   **Recommendation:** keep 42–45's union rule in 55 so 55 is directly comparable to the naive
   gao25 results it supersedes, and record the discrepancy prominently in the docstring. Offer
   aligning to 40's rule as a separate, explicitly-scoped change — do not silently switch it.

2. **`42`'s docstring names a file that does not exist:** `links/l23_evo/reguon_gene_table_gao25_v1l23.csv`
   (wrong directory, and "reguon" is a typo). The code reads
   `links/it/regulon_gene_table_gao25_v1l23.csv`. One-line docstring fix.

## Validation deliverables

1. **Universe robustness** per layer, both statistics — must reproduce the prototype table
   above (`log2_or` ~+0.95, `log2_enr` within ±0.1).
2. **Bin sensitivity** — `N_BINS` ∈ {10, 20, 40} × covariate ∈ {mean, detect, var}; median
   `log2_enr` should move <0.1 within a layer, as on yoo25.
3. **Closed-form vs resampling** — one `B = 20000` check per layer, as in 54.
4. **55 vs 42–45 agreement on the naive statistic.** 55 recomputes `log2_or` internally; assert
   it matches the committed `4X.gao25_<layer>_regulon_archetype_enrichment.tsv` to within
   floating-point tolerance on the shared (archetype, regulon) pairs. This is the check that the
   consolidation into one script changed nothing but the null — if it fails, the loader or the
   universe diverged and everything downstream is suspect.

## Open questions for the user

1. **Is the `54b`–`54e` parameterisation acceptable?** It touches four committed, working
   scripts. The alternative is duplication or a reduced gao25 figure set.
2. **Which figures does gao25 actually need?** 54's family grew to four because each was asked
   for in turn. If only the heatmap is wanted here, steps 4–5 shrink to one script.
3. **Should the `is_extended` rule be aligned across the two datasets?** Recommended as a
   separate change, but it is the single biggest obstacle to comparing yoo25 and gao25 regulons
   by name.
