# Human L4 IT — fit n=6 archetypes and compare against the canonical n=3

## Context

`scripts/it_evo/04.human_jorstad23_it_archetype_scores_loadings.py:73-74` fixes human L4 IT
at `noc: 3`, chosen at Gate B from the elbow in `ARV_mean` (0.029 at NOC=3 → 0.405 at NOC=4).
But `local_data/res/it_evo/03.refine.human_L4_num_archetype_metrics.tsv` is **not monotone**,
and NOC=6 is the sweep's best point:

| NOC | EV | ARV_mean ± std | ARV_rep | effEV_mean |
|-----|-------|-----------------|---------|------------|
| 2 | 0.408 | 0.008 ± 0.001 | 0.227 | 0.405 |
| 3 | 0.649 | 0.029 ± 0.003 | 0.203 | 0.630 |
| 4 | 0.789 | 0.405 ± 0.107 | 0.473 | 0.470 |
| 5 | 0.884 | 0.429 ± 0.090 | 0.238 | 0.505 |
| **6** | **0.961** | **0.200 ± 0.053** | **0.239** | **0.769** ← best |

NOC=6 has the highest `effEV_mean` and an `ARV_rep` (per-donor refit disagreement) equal to
NOC=2/3's. Human L4 has 8 `WithinArea_cluster` levels and 30,455 cells, so six extremes is
plausible a priori — L2/3 IT already carries four.

**Known caveats, accepted rather than tested here.** NOC=6 is exactly L4's structural ceiling
(`NOC ≤ ndim+1 = len(vx_cols) = 6`), where the fit is the minimal full-dimensional simplex and
EV is near-trivially high; and `get_relative_variation` (`src/scomics/utils.py:199-214`) aligns
replicate vertices only by `pcha()`'s PC1 sort (`utils.py:98`), with a denominator that grows
for a maximally-spread simplex. Either could manufacture the NOC=6 ARV dip. Deciding this
statistically would need a ceiling-control sweep; **we are skipping that and judging n=6 on
the biology of the fitted vertices instead** — markers, donor composition, and whether the six
vertices add territory over the three.

**Scope:** fit L4 at noc=6 and judge the result. One script, no comparison tooling — the
noc=3 fit is already on disk (`04.human_L4_*.tsv`) and can be read against the new outputs by
hand. No edits to `04`, `09`, `13`, `22` or `src/scomics/`.

---

## RESULTS — executed 2026-08-12: NOC=6 rejected, n=3 stands

Full write-up: `report/it_evo/04.human_L4_num_archetype_3_vs_6.md`. Steps 1 and 2 below were
carried out as planned; this section records what came back.

**Fit is valid.** The noc-independent self-check passed at **max |dev| = 0** on `pcha_xp`,
`pcha_inner_components` and `pcha_inner_mean`, so `04b` is a faithful copy of `04` and the two
fits are directly comparable. Top-cell sets stayed pairwise disjoint at 6 vertices, and every
vertex had markers (57 / 51 / 20 / 102 / 27 / 89), so neither of `04`'s kill signals fired.

### The killer: donor-specific artifact vertices

Max donor fraction of each vertex's 300 top cells, against a background split of
**0.354 / 0.334 / 0.311** (H19.30.002 / H19.30.001 / H18.30.002):

| noc=6 vertex | max donor | frac | dominant cluster | cluster frac |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| archetype_1 (A) | H18.30.002 | **0.897** ⚠ | L4 IT_5 | 0.953 |
| archetype_2 (B) | H18.30.002 | 0.597 | L4 IT_7 | 0.937 |
| archetype_3 (C) | H18.30.002 | 0.373 | L4 IT_2 | 0.687 |
| archetype_4 (D) | H19.30.002 | 0.427 | L4 IT_1 | 0.950 |
| archetype_5 (E) | H18.30.002 | 0.463 | L4 IT_3 | 1.000 |
| archetype_6 (F) | H19.30.001 | **0.920** ⚠ | L4 IT_8 | 1.000 |

Cross-tabulating `WithinArea_cluster` against `donor_id` and `Source` shows the two flagged
vertices fail by **two different mechanisms**, which is why neither is salvageable by a
threshold tweak:

- **archetype_6 — a confounded cluster.** It is 100% `L4 IT_8`, and `L4 IT_8` is itself
  **78.3% donor H19.30.001** and **59.1% `linnarson_10x_all_layers`** against a ~20% source
  background. Every other L4 cluster sits at 0.24–0.36 per donor and ~0.80/0.20 by source.
  This vertex is a donor/library artifact and would be indefensible carried into `09`'s
  cross-species comparison. **Note this is a *source* confound as much as a donor one — Gate A
  screened `donor_id` but never `Source`.**
- **archetype_1 — a within-cluster donor split.** Its dominant cluster `L4 IT_5` is
  donor-**balanced** (0.353 / 0.286 / 0.361), yet the vertex's top cells are 89.7% one donor.
  The vertex carves a donor axis *inside* a real cell type. This is the harder of the two to
  defend, and it is invisible to any cluster-level QC.

**Context that matters:** the canonical **noc=3 fit is donor-skewed too** — 0.733 (archetype_1,
H18.30.002) and 0.710 (archetype_3, H19.30.001). Donor skew is a property of human L4 at every
NOC, consistent with the elevated `ARV_rep` (0.20–0.24 at every NOC, vs 0.06–0.09 for L2/3)
already recorded at `04:70-72`. NOC=6 does not introduce the problem; it amplifies it to 0.90
and 0.92. Any future L4 work — including the existing n=3 fit — inherits this.

### Structure: a bisection, not new territory

Back-projected into shared VX space (`aa_vx = aa @ inner_comps + inner_mean`, `04:180`), each
noc=3 vertex splits into exactly two, and all three independent views agree:

```
noc6:  A  B  |  C  D  |  E  F
noc3:  1  1  |  2  2  |  3  3
```

Marker Jaccard puts each noc=3 vertex's two highest overlaps on the same pair (1→A/B
0.271/0.167; 2→C/D 0.167/0.143; 3→E/F 0.103/0.188); score correlation agrees (score_A→A/B
0.84/0.72; score_B→C/D 0.79/0.74; score_C→E/F 0.68/0.80).

The one point in NOC=6's favour: the splits are **not** near-duplicates — each vertex is
0.69–1.00 pure for a *different* `WithinArea_cluster`, taking cluster coverage from 3/8 to 6/8.
The solution finds real substructure. The problem is that two of the six clusters it resolves
are the donor/source-confounded ones.

### Decision

| criterion | outcome |
|---|---|
| Non-trivial markers on every vertex | PASS (min 20) |
| No donor-dominated vertex | **FAIL** — 0.897 and 0.920 |
| Adds territory rather than splitting | **FAIL** as a structural claim; mitigated by per-vertex cluster purity |

Criterion 2 alone is disqualifying. `04` keeps `noc: 3`; `09` / `13` / `22` unchanged. The
Gate-B comment at `04:70-74` now records that 6 was fitted and why it was rejected.

### What would change this

NOC=6 recovers real cluster substructure, so it becomes defensible if the donor/source
confound is removed rather than tolerated:

1. **Redo Gate A screening on `Source` as well as `donor_id`.** `02.human_L4_vx_variance_partition.tsv`
   reports a `source` partial R² per component and Gate A never used it — the `L4 IT_8`
   result says it should have.
2. Run the ceiling-control sweep skipped here (drop-one → cap 5, add-one → cap 7, plus
   Hungarian-matched ARV). If the ARV dip tracks the ceiling or vanishes under matching,
   NOC=6's statistical case disappears independently of the donor problem.

## Step 1 — `scripts/it_evo/04b.human_L4_noc6_scores_loadings.py` (the only new script)

A self-contained fork of `04`, L4 only, `NOC = 6`. Everything downstream of the fit copied
verbatim from `04...py:105-311` so the two fits are comparable: `PCHA_SEED = 0` re-seeded
immediately before `proj_and_pcha` (G8), `N_TOP_CELLS = 300`, same
`FRAC_IN_THRESH / FDR_THRESH / LOG2FC_THRESH`, same `SCORE_PCTILE_LO/HI`, same
`VX_COLS = ['VX3','VX4','VX5','VX6','VX8','VX10']`, `NDIM = 5`.

Deliberate deviations from `04`:

- **Drop the `pcha_gene_loadings` output** — nothing in the repo consumes
  `04.human_<TOKEN>_pcha_gene_loadings.tsv` (`16` explicitly declines it), so the fork
  should not carry it.
- **Record `04`'s two `WARNING` conditions as data.** A vertex with 0 markers is the primary
  kill signal for NOC=6, so per-vertex marker counts must land in a TSV, not just stdout.
- Keep both `raise` guards. `noc * 300 = 1800 ≤ 0.25 × 30455 = 7614`, fine; but the pairwise
  top-cell **disjointness** check may actually fire at 6 vertices — if it does, that firing
  *is* the answer and the script should stop there.
- **Add one small block: per-vertex composition** (~10 lines, no extra data load). For each
  vertex's 300 top cells, tabulate the `WithinArea_cluster` and `donor_id` distribution from
  the already-loaded `vx_df`. This is the decisive check and it is nearly free here: L4's
  `ARV_rep` is elevated at *every* NOC (0.20–0.24, vs 0.06–0.09 for L2/3), i.e. its three
  donors genuinely disagree more, so a vertex whose top cells are dominated by one donor is a
  donor artifact rather than a cell state. Background split is 0.354 / 0.334 / 0.311
  (H19.30.002 / H19.30.001 / H18.30.002).

## Step 2 — read the outputs and decide

No second script. The three artifacts that decide it are all produced by Step 1:

- `04b.human_L4_noc6_marker_counts.tsv` — does every vertex have a non-trivial marker set?
- `04b.human_L4_noc6_vertex_composition.tsv` — is any vertex donor-dominated relative to the
  0.354 / 0.334 / 0.311 background? How many of the 8 `WithinArea_cluster` levels show up as
  some vertex's dominant type?
- `04b.human_L4_noc6_archetype_scores.html` — do the six score gradients look like distinct
  states or like one gradient cut in two?

For a quick 3-vs-6 read, the canonical noc=3 files are already on disk and directly
comparable: the two fits share an identical inner PCA (see the self-check above), so
`aa_vx = aa.values @ inner_comps.values + inner_mean.values` (exactly `04:180`) puts both sets
of vertices in the same VX coordinates for an ad-hoc distance check, and
`04.human_L4_archetype_markers.tsv` gives the noc=3 marker sets to intersect against. If that
ad-hoc look turns out to need repeating, *then* it earns a script.

**Adopt n=6 only if all three hold:**
1. Every one of the six vertices has a non-trivial marker set, and the top-cell sets stayed
   disjoint (Step 1's guard did not fire).
2. No vertex is donor-dominated beyond the background donor split.
3. The six are not a relabelling of the three — they add territory rather than splitting one
   vertex into near-duplicates.

Either way, update the Gate-B comment at `04:70-74`, which currently justifies 3 solely on the
3→4 ARV break and never mentions NOC=6. That comment is the pipeline's record and must state
that 6 was fitted and what happened.

If n=6 wins, the follow-up (separate task): flip `04:74` to `noc: 6`, rerun `04 --tokens L4`
and `04.viz`, then update in lockstep `09` (`n_human` 3→6), `13` (`noc` 3→6; its 13 score
columns become 16) and `22` (`noc` 3→6; `22.human_IT_joint_archetype_arc_order.tsv` 13→16 rows,
which `13` reads back) — and delete `04b`; the fork is not a permanent second copy. The
skipped ceiling-control sweep would also need revisiting before publishing a 6-vertex L4.
