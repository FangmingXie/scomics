# Refitting the L2/3 axis pipeline at N_HVG = 4000

*Scripts: `it_evo/27`, `28`, `29`, `30`, `31` (all new).
Plan: `plan/it_evo/10.n_hvg_4000_refit.md`. Predecessor record: `report/it_evo/05`.*

## Question

The N=2000 HVG cut sits at the **minimum of the cross-species HVG enrichment curve** (1.41x
chance), with a secondary peak of 1.63x at N=4000. Does refitting both species' PCA+varimax
at N=4000 change the conserved axes?

Answered with a parallel record, not a migration: nothing existing was edited, every output
carries an `_hvg4000` tag, and the N=2000 record is untouched by construction.

## What was and was not refit

| refit at N=4000 | held fixed from N=2000 |
|---|---|
| human varimax (`27`), mouse varimax (`28`) | mouse archetypes and markers (`05`) |
| loading extension (`29`), CCA (`30`), figure (`31`) | archetype -> gene letter lookup |

The archetype labels are the N=2000 definitions, fitted in the N=2000 mouse Gate-A subspace
and held fixed while the axes are re-estimated. The reading is **"where do the previously
established mouse archetype programs sit on the newly estimated conserved axes?"** — this
record says nothing about whether the archetypes themselves are stable to gene selection.

Refitting them would have added zero coloured points: variance ranking is nested, so every
mouse marker is a mouse HVG at any N >= 2000, and `hvg_union` contains every mouse HVG.
Confirmed empirically — marker coverage is **A/B/C = 82/35/60 on `hvg_union` at both N**
(displayed as C'/B'/A' per the publication relabeling). `31` enforces this as a fixed target
rather than reporting it as an observation.

## The gene-set gate came in one gene low, and why that is fine

The plan gated on `hvg_intersect` = 1641 and `hvg_union` = 5486 from an independent sweep.
The pipeline produced **1640 / 5485** (`human_hvg` 3450 vs 3452; `mouse_hvg` 3675 matched
exactly).

Cause: the sweep accumulated gene variance in **float64**, whereas `02`/`it/19` — and so
`27`/`28`, which must copy them — cast to **float32** first. The rankings disagree on 3 genes
at the N=4000 cut, where the relative variance gap is ~1e-4, below float32 accumulation error
over 47k cells. Within the ortholog universe the difference is exactly `{ANKLE2, OPRD1,
ZNF697}` float64-only against `{PTDSS2}` float32-only — 3450 + 3 − 1 = 3452, reconciling both
figures to the gene. The plan had already recorded the same mechanism at N=2000 ("human
1999/2000, one float32 tie at the cut boundary"; that pair is `QSER1` vs `CNTNAP1`).

Matching `02`'s recipe outranks matching the sweep, so the gate now holds the pipeline
values. What the gate exists to catch — *drift* — is independently confirmed: `27`'s top-4000
reproduces the float32 ranking 4000/4000 and contains `02`'s stored top-2000 in full
(2000/2000), so the nesting the marker-saturation argument relies on is intact.

## Gate A had to be re-derived, and the new basis is cleaner

Varimax orders components by score variance, and that order changes with the gene set, so the
N=2000 VX numbering is meaningless here. Re-applying the original criterion to `27`/`28`'s
partition tables gives

- **human** `[VX3, VX6, VX8, VX9, VX10]` (kx=5)
- **mouse** `[VX1, VX3, VX4, VX6, VX9, VX10]` (ky=6)

so r = min(kx, ky) = 5. Full tables and rationale are in `plan/it_evo/10` §2. Every keeper has
`cell_type` as its largest factor by >=2.4x and every drop has a nuisance dominating by >=2.2x
— none of the near-ties that made human VX10 (donor .148 vs cell_type .131) and mouse VX9
(sample .137 vs .162) judgment calls at N=2000.

**One borderline call, measured rather than argued.** Human VX10 passes the partition test
(`cell_type` .196 beats every nuisance by >=5x) but carries 9 ribosomal-protein genes in its
top-50. Kept, because (i) it is the N=2000 VX8 situation (3 ribo genes, kept) and not the VX3
one (24 ribo genes *at ranks 1-2* **and** donor-dominated, dropped), its top-10 being entirely
biological; and (ii) `30.L23_axis_gate_sensitivity_hvg4000_union.tsv` shows dropping it moves
CCA1 from **0.3531 to 0.3530**, and its CCA1 weight is **0.036**. The choice is not
load-bearing. By contrast dropping human VX3 takes CCA1 to 0.268 and mouse VX1 takes it to
0.271 — those two carry the axis.

## Result: CCA1 is the same axis, better conditioned

Primary universe `hvg_union`, n=5485.

```
                 N=2000 (n=3220)      N=4000 (n=5485)
CCA1  r               0.358                0.353
      blocked-CV      0.312                0.329
      z                27.7                 37.2
      bootstrap  0.947 / 0.952        0.979 / 0.980
CCA2  r               0.313                0.256
      blocked-CV      0.307                0.233
      bootstrap  0.932 / 0.931        0.951 / 0.943
```

`r` is a property of the gene universe and **is not comparable across records** — the plan
predicted it would fall on dilution. It essentially did not: the *same* r now spans 5485 genes
instead of 3220, and every legitimately comparable quantity improves. CCA1's blocked-CV rises,
its permutation z rises, and its bootstrap weight stability rises to 0.98.

The universe ladder inside the N=4000 record shows the expected monotone dilution, with z
moving the other way — the signature of a real signal spread over more genes:

```
universe        n     CCA1 r    z     blocked-CV   boot
hvg_intersect  1640   0.481   24.6      0.432      0.97
human_hvg      3450   0.378   29.7      0.345      0.98
mouse_hvg      3675   0.393   33.3      0.362      0.98
hvg_union      5485   0.353   37.2      0.329      0.98
```

**CCA2 is the one thing that got worse.** Its r, blocked-CV and cross-N reproducibility all
fall. It remains bootstrap-stable and highly significant, but it is the weaker of the two
axes at N=4000 in a way it was not at N=2000.

## Verification

Canonical *weight* vectors are not comparable across records (different VX bases), so axis
identity was checked on the **per-gene canonical projections**, over the 3220 genes present in
both records:

```
            N=4000 vs N=2000       cross-axis |r| (human/mouse)
CCA1  human  r = +0.978            2k CCA1 x 4k CCA2 : 0.125 / 0.201
      mouse  r = +0.972            2k CCA2 x 4k CCA1 : 0.180 / 0.092
CCA2  human  r = +0.630
      mouse  r = +0.649
```

**CCA1 is the same axis re-expressed in a new basis** (|r| ~ 0.97-0.98), with low off-diagonal
correlation ruling out axis swapping. CCA2 at 0.63/0.65 is recognizably the same axis but
materially less reproducible across the gene set — consistent with its weaker CV and its
lower bootstrap median.

Negative control — permuting the ortholog pairing at n=5485 collapses the signal:

```
             CCA1 r   blocked-CV comp1   blocked-CV Scos2 excess
observed      0.353        +0.329               +0.293
perm 1        0.061        +0.019               -0.002
perm 2        0.053        +0.009               +0.000
perm 3        0.056        +0.014               +0.000
```

Other checks: `29`'s out-of-sample reconstruction reproduces the stored 4000-gene loadings to
1.8e-6 (human) and 1.7e-5 (mouse), against a 1e-4 tolerance; `31`'s panel r matches the
canonical correlation exactly; permutation p = 5e-5 (0/20000) for both axes; and no existing
script was edited.

## Reading the figure

`31.human_mouse_L23_gene_loadings_CCA{1,2}_hvg4000_union.pdf` differs from the N=2000 24b
figure only in its axes and gene set — the coloured points are the same 177 genes under the
same fixed labels, so any visual change is the axis. Archetype separation on CCA1 is
unambiguous in mouse (A' rb +0.76, C' rb −0.89) and attenuated but present in human (A' +0.33,
C' −0.37, B' ns), the same mouse-strong / human-weak asymmetry as the N=2000 figure.

**Pre-existing limitation:** the colouring is mouse-only, so the figure says where the *mouse*
archetype programs sit on the conserved axes and nothing about the human ones. A human
counterpart is a different figure and pulls `03`/`04` back in.

### The `hvg_intersect` companion

`31...CCA{1,2}_hvg4000.pdf` (no universe suffix) is the same figure on the 1640-gene double-HVG
intersection, r = 0.481 / 0.343. It carries **61 of 195 markers (internal A/B/C = 23/12/26)** —
exactly the count the plan's HVG-overlap sweep predicted for N=4000, an independent
confirmation that the gene set is built as intended.

It is a companion, not the primary: 61 coloured points against 177, and archetype B' fails to
separate from the bulk on CCA1 in either species (mouse rb −0.07 ns, human −0.24 ns) purely
for want of n — it separates strongly on the union figure (mouse rb +0.35 ***). This is the
same marker-attrition argument that motivated the universe switch in `report/it_evo/05`;
N=4000 narrows the gap but does not close it, which is why the plan kept the union.
