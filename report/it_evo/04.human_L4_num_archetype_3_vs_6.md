# Human L4 IT — NOC=6 fitted and rejected; n=3 stands

**Verdict: keep `noc=3`.** Two of the six NOC=6 vertices are donor artifacts, and the six are
a clean bisection of the three rather than new transcriptomic territory.

Script: `scripts/it_evo/04b.human_L4_noc6_scores_loadings.py` (exploratory fork of `04`).
Outputs: `local_data/res/it_evo/04b.human_L4_noc6_*`.

## Why NOC=6 was worth fitting

`03.refine.human_L4_num_archetype_metrics.tsv` is not monotone, and NOC=6 is the sweep's best
point on every summary metric:

| NOC | EV | ARV_mean ± std | ARV_rep | effEV_mean |
|-----|-------|-----------------|---------|------------|
| 2 | 0.408 | 0.008 ± 0.001 | 0.227 | 0.405 |
| 3 | 0.649 | 0.029 ± 0.003 | 0.203 | 0.630 |
| 4 | 0.789 | 0.405 ± 0.107 | 0.473 | 0.470 |
| 5 | 0.884 | 0.429 ± 0.090 | 0.238 | 0.505 |
| **6** | **0.961** | **0.200 ± 0.053** | **0.239** | **0.769** |

`04`'s Gate-B choice of 3 came from the 3→4 `ARV_mean` break (0.029 → 0.405) and never
addressed NOC=6, which has the highest `effEV_mean` in the sweep and an `ARV_rep`
indistinguishable from NOC=2/3. Human L4 has 8 `WithinArea_cluster` levels and 30,455 cells,
so six extremes was plausible a priori.

Two structural confounds were noted but **not** tested (no ceiling-control sweep was run):
NOC=6 is exactly L4's ceiling (`NOC ≤ NDIM+1 = len(VX_COLS) = 6`), where the fit is the
minimal full-dimensional simplex and EV is near-trivially high; and `get_relative_variation`
(`src/scomics/utils.py:199`) aligns replicate vertices only by `pcha()`'s PC1 sort, with a
denominator that grows for a maximally-spread simplex. The decision below rests on the
biology of the fitted vertices instead, and does not need those confounds resolved.

## Fit validity

The inner PCA is fitted by `proj(xf, ndim)` before PCHA runs, so `pcha_xp`,
`pcha_inner_components` and `pcha_inner_mean` do not depend on `noc`. All three reproduce
canonical `04.human_L4_*` at **max |dev| = 0**, so the fork is a faithful copy of `04` and the
two fits are directly comparable. Top-cell sets stayed pairwise disjoint at 6 vertices.

## Result 1 — every vertex has markers, but two are thin

| | A | B | C | D | E | F |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **noc=6** | 57 | 51 | **20** | 102 | **27** | 89 |
| **noc=3** | 159 | 106 | 145 | — | — | — |

No vertex is empty, so `04`'s zero-marker kill signal did not fire. Counts are not directly
comparable across NOC — the one-vs-each test is worst-case over `noc-1` comparisons, so 5
comparisons at NOC=6 vs 2 at NOC=3 is inherently more stringent — but C (20) and E (27) are
thin.

## Result 2 — two vertices are donor artifacts (the decisive finding)

Max donor fraction of each vertex's 300 top cells, against a background split of
0.354 / 0.334 / 0.311 (H19.30.002 / H19.30.001 / H18.30.002):

| noc=6 vertex | max donor | frac | dominant cluster | cluster frac |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| archetype_1 (A) | H18.30.002 | **0.897** | L4 IT_5 | 0.953 |
| archetype_2 (B) | H18.30.002 | 0.597 | L4 IT_7 | 0.937 |
| archetype_3 (C) | H18.30.002 | 0.373 | L4 IT_2 | 0.687 |
| archetype_4 (D) | H19.30.002 | 0.427 | L4 IT_1 | 0.950 |
| archetype_5 (E) | H18.30.002 | 0.463 | L4 IT_3 | 1.000 |
| archetype_6 (F) | H19.30.001 | **0.920** | L4 IT_8 | 1.000 |

Context matters here: **the canonical noc=3 fit is donor-skewed too** — 0.733 (archetype_1,
H18.30.002) and 0.710 (archetype_3, H19.30.001). Donor skew is a property of human L4 at every
NOC, consistent with the elevated `ARV_rep` (0.20–0.24 at every NOC, vs 0.06–0.09 for L2/3)
that `04:70-72` already records. What NOC=6 changes is the degree: 0.90 and 0.92.

Cross-tabulating cluster against donor identifies two distinct mechanisms:

- **archetype_6 is a batch artifact.** It is 100% `L4 IT_8`, and `L4 IT_8` is itself
  confounded: **78.3% donor H19.30.001** and **59.1% `linnarson_10x_all_layers`** against a
  ~20% source background. Every other L4 cluster sits at 0.24–0.36 per donor and 0.80/0.20 by
  source. This vertex is a donor/library artifact and would be indefensible carried into the
  cross-species comparison in `09`.
- **archetype_1 is a within-cluster donor split.** Its dominant cluster `L4 IT_5` is
  donor-balanced (0.353 / 0.286 / 0.361), yet the vertex's top cells are 89.7% H18.30.002. The
  vertex is picking a donor axis *inside* a real cell type, which is the harder failure to
  defend of the two.

## Result 3 — the six are a bisection of the three, not new territory

Back-projecting both fits into shared VX space (`aa_vx = aa @ inner_comps + inner_mean`,
`04:180`; valid because the inner PCA is identical), the nearest noc=3 vertex to each noc=6
vertex is:

```
noc6:  A  B  |  C  D  |  E  F
noc3:  1  1  |  2  2  |  3  3
```

Each noc=3 vertex splits into exactly two, and all three independent views agree. Marker
Jaccard puts each noc=3 vertex's two highest overlaps on the same pair (1→A/B 0.271/0.167;
2→C/D 0.167/0.143; 3→E/F 0.103/0.188), and score correlation does the same (score_A→A/B
0.84/0.72; score_B→C/D 0.79/0.74; score_C→E/F 0.68/0.80).

The one point in NOC=6's favour: the splits are not near-duplicates — each vertex is nearly
pure for a *different* `WithinArea_cluster` (L4 IT_5, _7, _2, _1, _3, _8 at 0.69–1.00), so 6 of
8 clusters become a dominant type versus 3 of 8 at noc=3. The extra vertices track the
existing taxonomy rather than noise. That is a real observation, but it is subordinate to
Result 2: two of the six clusters being tracked are the donor-confounded ones.

## Decision against the criteria

1. **Non-trivial markers on every vertex** — PASS (min 20).
2. **No donor-dominated vertex** — **FAIL**. archetype_1 at 0.897 and archetype_6 at 0.920,
   the latter on a cluster that is itself 78% one donor and 59% one source.
3. **Adds territory rather than splitting** — **FAIL as a structural claim** (clean 3→2
   bisection), partially mitigated by the per-vertex cluster purity.

Criterion 2 alone is disqualifying. **`04` keeps `noc: 3` for L4; `09`, `13` and `22` are
unchanged.**

## What would change this

The NOC=6 solution is not noise — it recovers real cluster substructure — so it becomes
defensible if the donor confound is removed rather than tolerated:

1. Re-examine Gate A. The VX subset was chosen to drop donor-loaded components (VX7 0.468,
   VX9 0.735), but donor structure evidently survives in the retained space. Since `L4 IT_8`
   is source- as well as donor-confounded, adding `Source` to the Gate-A screen — not just
   `donor_id` — is the obvious next move.
2. Run the ceiling-control sweep that was skipped: rerun the L4 NOC sweep under VX subsets
   whose `ndim+1` cap is not 6 (drop-one → cap 5, add-one → cap 7) and recompute ARV with
   Hungarian vertex matching. If the ARV dip tracks the ceiling or vanishes under matching,
   NOC=6's statistical case disappears independently of the donor problem.

`04b.human_L4_noc6_scores_loadings.py` is exploratory scaffolding, kept for the record. It is
not a pipeline stage and nothing reads its outputs.
