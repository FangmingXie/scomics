# VX3-Binned NR vs DR DEG Analysis — Cheng22 Astrocytes

**Dataset:** Cheng et al. 2022 mouse scRNA-seq (`cheng22_astro.h5ad`)  
**Cell populations:** NR (P28 + P38, 3,144 cells) and DR (P28_dr + P38_dr, 4,044 cells)  
**Scripts:** 44 · 45

---

## Context

Script 44 projected DR cells onto the NR Arch1+Arch2 varimax (VX) embedding space. VX3 is the third varimax component. To test whether visual-experience-dependent transcriptional changes (NR vs DR) are spatially graded along the VX3 axis, cells are divided into 4 equal-size bins along VX3 and an LMM is run within each bin.

---

## Procedures

### VX3 embedding (script 44)
- NR cells: directly from `41.cheng22_nr_harmony_arch12.h5ad` (`obsm['X_vx']`, column 2).
- DR cells: normalized with the same 2,000 HVGs and z-score statistics as NR; library size regressed out independently; then centered by NR `pca_mean` and projected via `vx_loadings`.
- Harmony correction is not applied to DR cells (it is donor-specific to NR samples).

### Binning (script 45)
- Cells are binned into **4 equal-size quantile bins along VX3 independently within each sample**, so every donor contributes ~equal cell fractions to each bin.

### DEG testing (script 45)
- Within each bin: LMM `expr ~ condition_code + (1 | sample)` where NR = 0, DR = 1.
- Expression: CP10k + log1p normalization.
- Gene filter: ≥ 10% of cells in the bin express the gene.
- FDR correction: Benjamini-Hochberg. Significance threshold: FDR < 0.05 **and |log2FC| > log2(1.5) ≈ 0.585**.
- Fallback optimizer chain: lbfgs → powell → nm.

### Rfx4 upregulation across VX3 bins

Rfx4 is a transcription factor known to regulate astrocyte identity and CNS development. Its DR-vs-NR profile across bins:

| Bin | log2FC | p-value | FDR | Note |
|-----|--------|---------|-----|------|
| 1 (low VX3) | −4.55 | 1.0 | 1.0 | Degenerate LMM fit — Rfx4 likely too sparse |
| 2 | +0.61 | 3.5×10⁻⁵ | 4.5×10⁻³ | Significant (up in DR) |
| 3 | −0.81 | 1.0 | 1.0 | Degenerate LMM fit — Rfx4 likely too sparse |
| 4 (high VX3) | +0.79 | 2.3×10⁻¹¹ | 2.1×10⁻⁹ | Significant (up in DR) |

Rfx4 is significantly upregulated in DR cells in Bins 2 and 4 (both pass FDR < 0.05 and |log2FC| > log2(1.5) ≈ 0.585). Bins 1 and 3 return degenerate fits (p = 1.0 with extreme log2FC artifacts), consistent with Rfx4 being too lowly or variably expressed in those cell populations for the LMM to converge. The consistent upregulation in the two bins where it is estimable suggests Rfx4 induction is a broad feature of the DR transcriptional response, not confined to the high-VX3 Arch1-enriched state.

---

## Conclusions

- With a |log2FC| > log2(1.5) cutoff, the NR-vs-DR transcriptional difference is overwhelmingly concentrated in **Bin 4** (70 DEGs vs 5–8 in Bins 1–3), confirming that large-effect visual-experience-dependent changes are specific to the high-VX3 (Arch1-enriched) state.
- **Bin 4 is DR-dominated** (54 up in DR vs 16 up in NR), in contrast to Bins 1–3 where the small number of DEGs are roughly balanced or NR-leaning — suggesting that dark-rearing actively upregulates a broad transcriptional program in Arch1-like cells.
- **Recurring genes across bins**: Gem (GTPase, up in DR) appears in Bins 2, 3, and 4 with increasing effect size (+0.66 → +0.85 → +1.03); Hmgn3 (chromatin regulator, up in NR) appears in Bins 1 (sub-threshold), 3, and 4, suggesting these are pan-VX3 visual-dependent genes.
- **Bin 4-specific large-effect hits**: Eprs (+1.54, aminoacyl-tRNA synthetase), Arhgap31 (+1.16, Rho GAP), Btbd9 (+0.97) up in DR; Wwc1 (−0.72, Hippo pathway), St3gal4 (−0.70, sialylation), Pik3r1 (−0.71, PI3K regulatory subunit) up in NR — implicating protein synthesis capacity, cytoskeletal remodeling, and signaling pathway regulation as the primary axes of visual-experience-dependent change in mature astrocytes.
