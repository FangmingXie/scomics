# VX3-Binned NR vs DR DEG Analysis — Pseudobulk + DESeq2 (v4) — Cheng22 Astrocytes

**Dataset:** Cheng et al. 2022 mouse scRNA-seq (`cheng22_astro.h5ad`)
**Cell populations:** NR (P28 + P38, 3,144 cells) and DR (P28_dr + P38_dr, 4,044 cells)
**Replication:** 7 NR mice, 8 DR mice
**Scripts:** 44 · 45.v4 (`45.v4.deg_vx3_bins_nr_vs_dr_deseq2.py`)
**Supersedes:** `45.deg...md` (v1, cell-level LMM), `45.v2...md` (hardened LMM)

---

## Context

VX3 is the third varimax component of the NR Arch1+Arch2 embedding; DR cells are projected into it (script 44). Cells are split into 4 equal-size VX3 bins (within each sample) to test whether the NR-vs-DR (visual-experience) transcriptional response is spatially graded along VX3.

This analysis chain went through three method generations because the test, not the biology, kept changing the answer:

| Ver | Method | Problem it exposed |
|-----|--------|--------------------|
| v1 | cell-level LMM `expr ~ cond + (1\|sample)` | bins 1–3 polluted by FDR≈1 / huge-FC artifacts; Rfx4 sign flips |
| v2 | hardened LMM (filter unstable/singular fits) | revealed bins 1–3 are ~78% **numerically degenerate** (statsmodels variance-boundary, SE≈10⁶), collapsing them to 4–7 DEGs — an artifact, since effect size, cell count, and between-mouse ICC (≈0.016) are matched across all 4 bins |
| v3 | pseudobulk + t-test / Wilcoxon | fixes identifiability; but unmoderated t-test is anticonservative at n=7/8 and Wilcoxon has ~0 power (min achievable FDR ≈ 0.07) |
| **v4** | **pseudobulk + DESeq2** | **calibrated:** NB-GLM with dispersion shrinkage + apeglm LFC shrinkage |

**v4 is the recommended analysis.** Pseudobulk aggregates raw counts per (sample × bin) → one profile per mouse per bin (7 NR + 8 DR), making the mouse the unit of replication and removing the per-gene random-effect boundary entirely. DESeq2 then moderates dispersion across genes (the key to calibrated FDR at low replicate count) and shrinks noisy LFCs.

---

## Procedures

### Pseudobulk + DESeq2 (script 45.v4)
- Binning identical to scripts 45 / 45.v2 / 45.v3 (4 equal-size VX3 quantile bins within each sample), computed on the normalized `adata.X` embedding.
- **Counts source:** `adata.X` in this h5ad is *normalized* (fractional values, max ≈ 6.7) and is used only for the VX embedding/binning. DESeq2 requires raw integer counts, which live in **`adata.raw`** (integer, max ≈ 1256, per-cell median depth ≈ 3,500). The pseudobulk is built from `adata.raw[:, adata.var_names].X`. *(An earlier version of this script mistakenly summed and rounded the normalized `adata.X`; the numbers below are from the corrected raw-count version.)*
- **Pseudobulk:** sum raw integer counts across cells for each (sample × bin); drop a profile with < 10 cells (none dropped — every mouse contributes ~1/4 of its cells per bin).
- **Per bin:** DESeq2 (`pydeseq2` 0.5.2) with design `~condition`, contrast DR vs NR (positive log2FC = up in DR).
  - Gene pre-filter: total count ≥ 10 across the bin's pseudobulk samples (~13.6k of 16.6k genes kept).
  - Size-factor normalization, NB-GLM, gene-wise dispersion shrinkage, Wald test, Cook's + independent filtering for `padj`.
  - **apeglm-style LFC shrinkage** applied; `log2FC` is the shrunk estimate (used for ranking/threshold), `log2FC_mle` is the unshrunk MLE (reported alongside).
- Significance: `padj` (FDR) < 0.05 **and |shrunk log2FC| > log2(1.5) ≈ 0.585**.
- Output columns: `gene, log2FC, log2FC_mle, baseMean, lfcSE, stat, pval, fdr, n_NR, n_DR`.

> Note on LFC scale: DESeq2 LFCs are on the count (NB-GLM) scale and are larger in magnitude than the cell-level `log1p(CP10k)` mean differences from v1/v2 (e.g. Nr4a2 ≈ +3.4 here vs ≈ +1.25 in v2). Sign and ranking are what transfer across methods, not absolute magnitude.

## Results

### DEG counts per bin (FDR < 0.05 & |log2FC_shrink| > log2(1.5))

| Bin | VX3 range | Sig DEGs | up in DR | up in NR |
|-----|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| 1 (low VX3) | [−6.77, −0.83] | 574 | 442 | 132 |
| 2 | [−2.50, 0.92] | 505 | 387 | 118 |
| 3 | [−0.99, 2.48] | 598 | 437 | 161 |
| 4 (high VX3) | [0.33, 7.40] | **607** | 434 | 173 |

**The headline change vs v1/v2:** with a properly calibrated test, the NR-vs-DR response is **broad across all four VX3 bins** (505–607 DEGs each), not concentrated in bin 4. The earlier "bin-4-specific" conclusion was largely an artifact of the cell-level LMM silently failing in bins 1–3. All bins are strongly **DR-skewed** (~72–77% of DEGs up in DR), consistent with dark-rearing actively inducing a transcriptional program.

## Conclusions

- **Method matters more than the bins.** v4 (pseudobulk + DESeq2) is the calibrated answer; v1/v2's bin-4-specificity was largely a cell-level-LMM numerical artifact.
- **The NR-vs-DR response is broad along VX3** (505–607 DEGs per bin, all DR-skewed), with a shared ~128-gene core plus bin-enriched layers.
- **The core DR program is an immediate-early / activity-regulated signature** — Fos, Fosl2, Junb, Egr4, Nr4a1/2/3, Btg2, Homer1 — induced in astrocytes across all VX3 bins by dark-rearing. Hmgn3/Wwc1/Gm3764 are the consistent up-in-NR genes.
- **Cross-method-robust hits** (significant by LMM-ok, pseudobulk t-test, and DESeq2): Eprs, Nr4a1/2, Adamts9, Gem, Arhgap31, Man1b1, Rfx4 (all bins) up in DR; Hmgn3, Wwc1 up in NR.
- **Counts caveat (fixed):** DESeq2 must run on raw counts; the raw integer matrix lives in `adata.raw` (not `adata.X`, which is normalized). An earlier version summed the normalized `adata.X`; results here are from the corrected raw-count version (it raised counts modestly — e.g. bin totals ~505–607 vs ~364–503 — and strengthened Rfx4 to significance in all bins).
- **Other caveats:** (i) ~500–600 DEGs/bin at n=7/8 is well-powered but assumes NR vs DR are not confounded by experimental batch — a large shared component could partly reflect batch; orthogonal validation is warranted. (ii) IEGs (Fos/Jun/Egr/Nr4a) are also classic dissociation/handling artifacts — their induction should be interpreted with that caveat unless protocol was matched between NR and DR.
