# VX3-Binned NR vs DR DEG Analysis — Hardened LMM (v2) — Cheng22 Astrocytes

**Dataset:** Cheng et al. 2022 mouse scRNA-seq (`cheng22_astro.h5ad`)
**Cell populations:** NR (P28 + P38, 3,144 cells) and DR (P28_dr + P38_dr, 4,044 cells)
**Replication:** 7 NR mice, 8 DR mice (condition is a well-replicated between-subjects factor)
**Scripts:** 44 · 45 · **45.v2** (`45.v2.deg_vx3_bins_nr_vs_dr_lmm_hardened.py`)
**Supersedes:** `45.deg_vx3_bins_nr_vs_dr.md` (v1)

---

## Context

Script 44 projected DR cells onto the NR Arch1+Arch2 varimax (VX) embedding space. VX3 is the third varimax component. To test whether visual-experience-dependent transcriptional changes (NR vs DR) are spatially graded along the VX3 axis, cells are divided into 4 equal-size bins along VX3 and an LMM is run within each bin.

The original analysis (v1, script 45) ran a cell-level LMM `expr ~ condition + (1|sample)` per bin. Visualizing those results revealed a systematic artifact: in bins 1–3, thousands of genes carried **large fold changes with FDR ≈ 1**, and individual genes (e.g. **Rfx4**) showed log2FC values that contradicted the raw per-cell expression — including impossible sign flips (Rfx4 = −4.55 in bin 1 while raw expression is *higher* in DR). The cause is **per-gene degenerate LMM fits**: with condition fully nested in mouse and many sparse genes, individual fits become non-identifiable (huge fixed-effect SE → p ≈ 1) or singular (random-effect variance collapses to 0). v1 took the first optimizer that returned a number, so these unreliable estimates leaked into the output.

**v2 keeps the cell-level LMM** — it is the correct model given the replication — but hardens the per-gene fitting so degenerate fits are detected and excluded rather than reported as hits.

---

## Procedures

### VX3 embedding (script 44) — unchanged
- NR cells: directly from `41.cheng22_nr_harmony_arch12.h5ad` (`obsm['X_vx']`, column 2).
- DR cells: normalized with the same 2,000 HVGs and z-score statistics as NR; library size regressed out independently; then centered by NR `pca_mean` and projected via `vx_loadings`.
- Harmony correction is not applied to DR cells (it is donor-specific to NR samples).

### Binning (script 45/45.v2) — unchanged
- Cells are binned into **4 equal-size quantile bins along VX3 independently within each sample**, so every donor contributes ~equal cell fractions to each bin.

### DEG testing — hardened (script 45.v2)
- Within each bin: LMM `expr ~ condition_code + (1 | sample)` where NR = 0, DR = 1. Expression: CP10k + log1p.
- **Hardening changes vs v1:**
  1. **Stricter expression filter** — gene must be expressed in **≥ 25%** of cells in the bin (v1: 10%). Drops ultra-sparse genes whose coefficients are wild.
  2. **Convergence required** — the optimizer chain (lbfgs → powell → nm) now **prefers a genuinely converged fit**; a fit that returns numbers without converging is flagged, not silently accepted.
  3. **Fit-quality flags** per gene, recorded in a `status` column:
     - `unstable_se` — fixed-effect SE > 3 (log units): effect non-identifiable, p ≈ 1.
     - `singular_re` — random-effect variance / residual variance < 1×10⁻³: random intercept collapsed to ~OLS (pseudoreplication → anticonservative p).
     - `sign_mismatch` — model log2FC sign disagrees with the raw NR-vs-DR mean difference (|raw| > 0.05).
     - `not_converged` / `no_fit` — optimizer failures.
     - `ok` — passes all checks.
  4. **FDR computed only over `ok` genes** (Benjamini-Hochberg), so the multiple-testing correction is not diluted by untrustworthy tests.
  5. **Raw per-condition means and raw log2FC reported** (`mean_NR`, `mean_DR`, `raw_log2FC`) alongside the model estimate, so model vs data can always be cross-checked.
- Significance threshold (unchanged): FDR < 0.05 **and |log2FC| > log2(1.5) ≈ 0.585**, restricted to `ok` genes.
- Output columns: `gene, log2FC, raw_log2FC, mean_NR, mean_DR, pval, fdr, se, re_var_rel, converged, status`.

### Fit-quality breakdown (the hardening in action)

Per-bin status of the genes passing the ≥25% expression filter:

| Bin | genes tested | `ok` | `unstable_se` | `singular_re` | other |
|-----|--------------|------|---------------|---------------|-------|
| 1 (low VX3) | 2,168 | 358 | 1,696 | 114 | — |
| 2 | 2,365 | 365 | 1,868 | 132 | — |
| 3 | 2,403 | 384 | 1,874 | 145 | — |
| 4 (high VX3) | 2,414 | **1,729** | 0 | 684 | 1 no_fit |

The breakdown is itself a key result: in **bins 1–3, ~78% of genes are `unstable_se`** — non-identifiable fits that v1 reported as large-fold-change, p ≈ 1 "hits." **Bin 4 has zero unstable fits** (1,729 of 2,414 genes are `ok`). The condition effect is only cleanly estimable at the high-VX3 end, which is exactly where the real signal lives.

## Conclusions

- **The artifact is resolved.** Hardening removes the FDR ≈ 1 / large-fold-change cloud and the sign-flipped pseudo-hits (Rfx4 bins 1/3). In every reported DEG, model log2FC now agrees with the raw per-cell mean difference.
- **The fit-quality breakdown is biologically informative.** Bins 1–3 are dominated by non-identifiable fits (~78% `unstable_se`); Bin 4 has none. The NR-vs-DR contrast is only cleanly estimable in the high-VX3 (Arch1-enriched) state.
- **Signal is concentrated in Bin 4** (60 DEGs, DR-skewed 44:16; all 8 large-effect |log2FC|>1 hits up in DR) versus 4–7 DEGs in Bins 1–3 — the same conclusion as v1, now resting on trustworthy fits.
- **Recurring genes** track VX3: Gem (up in DR) appears in bins 2/3/4 with increasing effect (+0.85 → +0.85 → +1.03); Hmgn3 (up in NR) in bins 1/3/4; Egr1 (up in DR) in bins 1/2; Adamts9 in bins 1/4; Aldh1a1 (up in NR) in bins 2/4.
- **Bin-4 program:** protein synthesis (Eprs, Slc3a2, Rheb), activity-regulated TFs (Nr4a1/2/3, Junb, Egr-family), cytoskeletal/structural remodeling (Gem, Arhgap31, Tpm3, Emd), and lipid/sterol metabolism (Hmgcr, Elovl5, Dhrs3) up in DR; Hippo (Wwc1), PI3K (Pik3r1), glutamate metabolism (Glud1, Glul), and hypoxia (Hif3a) up in NR.
- **Caveat — `singular_re`:** Bin 4 has 684 genes whose random-effect variance collapsed to ~0; these are excluded from significance because their p-values would be anticonservative (pseudoreplication). They may include real effects and are candidates for orthogonal confirmation (e.g. pseudobulk DESeq2/edgeR).
